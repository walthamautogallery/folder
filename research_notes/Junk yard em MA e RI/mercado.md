# US Auto Salvage / Auto Recycling / Junk Yard Market and Business Models (focus on MA and RI), 2025-2026

Research date: 2026-11-24. Every number is dated. "Search summary" means the figure came from a search-result summary of the cited page and the page itself was not opened (several primary pages returned HTTP 403); those should be re-verified before being quoted as exact.

## 1. Industry size and structure

### Takeaway
US auto recycling is a large (ARA: ~$32B/yr), highly fragmented industry of ~9,000 locations handling ~12M+ vehicles a year, where the narrower "used car parts wholesaling" slice measured by IBISWorld is ~$9-12B with ~1,450 businesses and low concentration (LKQ is the largest). The typical yard sold on the open market is a ~$1.1M-revenue small business with ~$250K owner earnings.

### Cited Findings
- ARA states the automotive recycling industry is the 16th largest in the US, valued at an estimated $32 billion annually (undated on ARA page; search summary). — [ARA Industry Statistics](https://www.a-r-a.org/industry-statistics/)
- ARA: automotive recycling businesses employ over 140,000 people at more than 9,000 locations in the US (undated; search summary). — [ARA Industry Statistics](https://www.a-r-a.org/industry-statistics/)
- ARA's newer page says automotive recyclers process over 12 million vehicles per year; an older ARA page cites "over 4 million motor vehicles annually in the US and Canada" (the two ARA pages conflict; the 12M figure is the newer one; search summary). — [ARA About Us](https://www.a-r-a.org/about-us/); contradicted by older [ARA Industry Statistics](https://www.a-r-a.org/industry-statistics/)
- IBISWorld, Used Car Parts Wholesaling in the US: industry revenue ~$12.2B in 2026, CAGR 4.9% 2021-2026 (search summary of public teaser; full report paywalled). — [IBISWorld Used Car Parts Wholesaling](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/industry/used-car-parts-wholesaling/916/)
- An earlier IBISWorld edition (updated Feb 2025) estimated revenue of $9.3B in 2025 with 3.4% CAGR over five years — conflicts with the $12.2B/2026 figure above, likely an edition/revision difference (search summary). — [IBISWorld market size page](https://www.ibisworld.com/industry-statistics/market-size/used-car-parts-wholesaling-united-states/)
- IBISWorld: 1,443 used car parts wholesaling businesses in the US in 2025 (-0.3% vs 2024); 1,454 in 2026 (+0.8% vs 2025) (search summary). — [IBISWorld Number of Businesses](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/number-of-businesses/used-car-parts-wholesaling/916)
- IBISWorld: industry is made up largely of regional, independent dealers; low market-share concentration; the largest business is LKQ Corporation (search summary). — [IBISWorld Used Car Parts Wholesaling](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/industry/used-car-parts-wholesaling/916/)
- Junk Car Medics (secondary source, not primary data) reports that 86% of auto recyclers employ 10 or fewer people (undated). — [Junk Car Medics statistics](https://www.junkcarmedics.com/?p=7329)
- BizBuySell sold-business comps for junk and salvage yards, 2021-2025: median revenue $1,141,763; median owner earnings $251,000; median sale price ~$1.10M (25th-75th percentile ~$519K-$1.43M); median earnings multiple 3.85x; median revenue multiple 1.03x. The page also shows unlabeled "0.98" and "287" (probably sale-to-ask ratio and days on market) and an internally inconsistent top-quartile earnings figure (search summary; page returned 403 on direct fetch). — [BizBuySell valuation benchmarks: junk/salvage yard](https://www.bizbuysell.com/learning-center/valuation-benchmarks/junk-salvage-yard/)

### Inferences
- BizBuySell median owner earnings / median revenue = roughly 22% seller's-discretionary-earnings margin for a typical small yard (owner's salary included, so true net margin is lower). This is my arithmetic from the two medians, which may not be from the same businesses.
- ARA's $32B vs IBISWorld's ~$9-12B is a scope difference, not a contradiction: ARA counts the whole recycling chain; IBISWorld's NAICS-based code counts only used-parts wholesalers (~1,450 enterprises vs ARA's 9,000+ locations).
- The ARA headline figures ($32B, 140,000 employees, 9,000 locations) have circulated unchanged for years and should be treated as order-of-magnitude, not 2025-2026 measurements.

### Gaps
- No verified 2025-2026 figure for ELVs retired per year (the commonly quoted 12-15M range was only confirmed at "over 12 million" via ARA).
- IBISWorld profit margin, employment and wage data for the industry are paywalled; not retrieved.
- IBISWorld "Scrap Metal Recycling" and "Auto Parts Wholesaling" figures were not retrieved.
- No count of licensed salvage yards specifically in Massachusetts or Rhode Island was found.

## 2. Business models and their economics

### Takeaway
The only audited look at self-service (U-Pull-It) economics is LKQ's Self Service segment: $532M revenue in 2024, 42.7% gross margin, but only 9.3% segment EBITDA margin, and very exposed to scrap and precious-metal prices. Independent, citable unit economics (revenue per vehicle, parts-vs-scrap split, turnover days, cars per acre) were not found in reliable sources.

### Cited Findings
- Self-service model (LKQ Self Service / Pick Your Part), FY2024: revenue $532M (down from $597M in 2023); segment EBITDA $50M = 9.3% margin (vs $36M = 6.0% in 2023) (search summary of 10-K). — [LKQ FY2024 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065696/000106569625000015/lkq-20241231.htm)
- LKQ Self Service gross margin rose to 42.7% in 2024 from 37.2% in 2023, attributed to improved vehicle procurement costs, partly offset by lower commodity prices (search summary of 10-K). — [LKQ FY2024 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065696/000106569625000015/lkq-20241231.htm)
- Commodity sensitivity at LKQ Self Service, 2024: lower precious-metal prices cut gross margin by an estimated $13M year over year; scrap steel had a $2M unfavorable effect in 2024 vs a $5M favorable effect in 2023 (search summary of 10-K). — [LKQ FY2024 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065696/000106569625000015/lkq-20241231.htm)
- Self-service yards charge a nominal admission fee: an Indiana operator reported $1 to enter, which it said helps offset environmental costs (Recycling Today, older article, undated in results); a 2008 Honolulu report also cites $1 admission and flat per-part pricing regardless of make. Both data points are old. — [Recycling Today, "Staying Focused"](https://recyclingtoday.com/article/staying-focused); [Honolulu Advertiser 2008](https://archives-new.honoluluadvertiser.com/article/2008/Jul/05/bz/hawaii807050318.html)
- Qualitative comparison (vendor blog, not independent): self-service needs less dismantling equipment and fewer staff because customers pull parts; full-service can generate significantly greater revenue per vehicle from professionally dismantled parts when repair demand is strong; many operators run hybrids (page returned 403 on direct fetch; search summary only, no numbers). — [Hub Industrial, self-service vs full-service](https://www.hubindustrial.com/resources/auto-recycling-news-and-resources/self-service-vs-full-service-recycling-models-which-delivers-better-margins-in-auto-recycling.html)
- Catalytic converters and buying strategy (July 13, 2023 article by Becky Berube, United Catalyst Corp.): most full-serve yards do NOT include converter value in the price they pay for a car; most self-serve yards DO, because cars are "basically priced the same at auction" and knowing the converter value gives a buying edge. Average converter price was "slightly north of $100" in mid-2023, vs ~$50 a decade earlier and a peak of ~$350. — [Auto Recycling World, "The Commodity of Cats"](https://autorecyclingworld.com/the-commodity-of-cats-full-serve-and-self-serve-yards/)
- Scrap-oriented / "cash for junk cars" buying, 2026: Junk Car Medics (Feb 2026 release) quotes scrap rates of $120-$200 per ton and average vehicle payouts of $100-$600; its April 2026 page lists an average payout of $512 over the prior 30 days. — [Junk Car Medics release, Feb 2026](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/245025/junk-car-medics-announces-updated-2026-scrap-car-pricing-and-increased-payouts-nationwide); [Junk Car Medics prices](https://www.junkcarmedics.com/prices)
- Other buyer-run indices, 2026: Junk Car Reaper national average $190/ton and average payout $475 (Feb 2026); JunkUrCar Fall 2026 index median offer $350 (middle half $250-$525) across 42,268 offers; Cash Auto Salvage average payout $400, top 10% ~$1,090. All are marketing content from buyers, not independent data. — [Junk Car Reaper](https://www.junkcarreaper.com/junk-car-prices); [JunkUrCar index](https://news.marketersmedia.com/junk-car-prices-by-state-2026-median-offer-is-dollar350/89207453); [Cash Auto Salvage](https://www.cashautosalvage.com/going-rate-junk-cars/)
- Fenix Parts (full-service OEM recycled parts consolidator) also runs self-service: its Brothers Auto Parts (Indianapolis) deal took it to 24 service locations and added five self-service sites (date not in results). — [Aftermarket News](https://www.aftermarketnews.com/fenix-parts-acquires-brothers-auto-parts/)

### Inferences
- LKQ's numbers imply self-service is a high-gross-margin, low-net-margin, fixed-cost-heavy business: 42.7% gross margin collapses to 9.3% EBITDA after land, labor, towing and environmental costs, and a $13M swing in precious metals equals about a quarter of segment EBITDA. If the largest, most scaled operator earned 6-9% EBITDA, a sub-scale new entrant should not plan on better.
- An ELV bought at ~$350-$500 (2026 buyer indices) and worth $120-$200/ton as scrap (~1.5 tons) has scrap value of only ~$180-$300; the acquisition cost is only recovered through parts, converter, wheels, battery and cores. This is my arithmetic, not a sourced figure.
- The converter is treated as part of the purchase price in self-service and as upside in full-service, so self-service margins are structurally more tied to PGM prices.

### Gaps
- No reliable source found for: revenue per vehicle by model, parts-vs-scrap revenue split, inventory turnover (45-90 days), cars per acre, or typical flat price lists. These are widely repeated in the trade but I could not source them; treat as unverified.
- No sourced data on full-service economics (average part price, parts per vehicle, Hollander/Pinnacle/Powerlink/Checkmate software costs, car-part.com or eBay Motors fees, shipping share).
- No sourced data on rebuildable/export hybrid model economics.
- Capital requirements, MA licensing costs, land prices and environmental compliance costs were out of scope for this search and not found.

## 3. Major players and consolidation

### Takeaway
2025 was a reshuffling year: LKQ exited self-service (sold Pick Your Part to private equity for $410M) and Radius/Schnitzer (owner of Pick-n-Pull) was taken private by Toyota Tsusho for ~$907M. Strategic and PE capital is consolidating the industry, while the largest public company judged self-service non-core.

### Cited Findings
- LKQ agreed on Aug 26, 2025 to sell its Self Service segment (Pick Your Part) to an affiliate of Pacific Avenue Capital Partners for $410M enterprise value, after a competitive bid process with several interested parties; proceeds to repay debt. — [LKQ 8-K press release, Aug 26, 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065696/000106569625000051/exhibit991-selfservice.htm)
- The sale closed Oct 1, 2025; Self Service is reported as discontinued operations from Q3 2025. CEO Justin Jude said the business no longer aligns with LKQ's long-term strategy (search summary). — [LKQ completion release](https://lkqcorp.com/blog/news/lkq-corporation-completes-the-sale-of-its-self-service-segment/)
- The unit had 2024 revenue of $532M and was 3.5% of LKQ's Q2 2025 revenue (search summary). — [MDM](https://www.mdm.com/news/mergers-acquisitions/auto-parts-distributor-lkq-to-sell-self-service-unit-in-410m-private-deal/)
- Toyota Tsusho America agreed in March 2025 to buy Radius Recycling (formerly Schnitzer Steel) for $30.00 per share in cash, roughly $907M; closed July 10, 2025; Radius was delisted from Nasdaq and keeps its Portland HQ, management, facilities and brand. — [Radius 8-K, July 10, 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/912603/000114036125025485/ef20051635_ex99-1.htm); [Waste Dive](https://www.wastedive.com/news/toyota-tsusho-radius-recycling-acquisition-scrap-metal/742671/)
- Radius's self-service auto parts business, Pick-n-Pull, had 50 stores in the US and Western Canada at the time of the deal (2025). — [Waste Dive](https://www.wastedive.com/news/toyota-tsusho-radius-recycling-acquisition-scrap-metal/742671/)
- Fenix Parts (sponsor-backed; sources name Stellex Capital, one names "Stellar") keeps buying full-service recyclers: Assured Auto Parts, San Antonio TX (April 2025); Grade A Auto Parts, Louisville KY, with locations in KY, MO, OH, TN, IN (Aug 2025). — [CollisionWeek](https://collisionweek.com/2025/04/08/fenix-parts-acquires-auto-recycler-san-antonio-texas/); [ARA](https://www.a-r-a.org/?p=1671)
- Copart (auction side) operates over 250 locations in 11 countries and sold more than 4 million units in the latest year (FY2025, secondary summary of 10-K). — [TradingView summary of Copart 10-K](https://www.tradingview.com/news/tradingview%3A98591ffae26cc%3A0-copart-inc-sec-10-k-report)

### Inferences
- $410M / $532M = ~0.77x 2024 revenue and $410M / $50M = ~8.2x 2024 segment EBITDA for the largest US self-service chain (my arithmetic; LKQ did not disclose multiples). That is far above the ~3.85x earnings multiple of small yards on BizBuySell — scale is valued, single yards are not.
- An automaker-affiliated trading house buying a shredder/self-service group signals OEM interest in securing recycled metal supply; this can support demand for hulks over time but also strengthens an already dominant buyer of shredder feed.

### Gaps
- Number and states of Pick Your Part locations, and vehicles processed per year, were not in the documents retrieved.
- Nothing found on Pull-A-Part, IAA/RB Global figures, or the online buyers Peddle, CarBrain and Wheelzy (volumes, funding, market share).
- No 2026 M&A activity confirmed.

## 4. New England competitive landscape (MA / RI)

### Takeaway
Greater Boston's scrap outlet is concentrated: Radius (ex-Schnitzer) runs the Everett, MA shredder and export dock, with only two other shredders in Massachusetts, and Sims shreds in Johnston, RI and exports through Providence. No national self-service chain (Pick Your Part, Pick-n-Pull) was found in MA or RI; self-service appears to be offered only by a handful of local family yards.

### Cited Findings
- Massachusetts has three metal shredding plants: Radius Recycling in Everett, wTe Recycling in Greenfield, and Excel Recycling in Freetown (search summary of MassDEP material). — [MassDEP presentation](https://www.mass.gov/doc/presentation-eri/download)
- The Everett shredder was out of service after a fire on Dec 8, 2021 and restarted Jan 28, 2022. — [Business Wire, Feb 1, 2022](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220201006095/en/Schnitzer-Restarts-Shredding-Operations-at-Everett-MA-Facility)
- A later Everett outage lasted nearly five months (restart in the second week of November; year not given in results). Argus reported that during the outage New England shredder feedstock prices (such as auto hulks) "plummeted and have remained depressed compared with pricing in other markets". — [Recycling Today](https://www.recyclingtoday.com/news/schnitzer-massachusetts-auto-metal-shredding-recycling-restart)
- Fastmarkets publishes a specific price series for auto bodies delivered to export yards in Boston ($/gross ton), confirming Boston is an export-priced market for car hulks (price values are paywalled). — [Fastmarkets MB-STE-0370](https://www.fastmarkets.com/commodity-prices/steel-scrap-auto-bodies-export-yard-buying-price-delivered-to-yard-boston-dollar-gross-ton-mb-ste-0370/)
- Sims entered the Port of Providence in 2011 by buying Promet Marine's deep-water export facility, planning to shred autos in Johnston, RI and ship via Providence. — [Recycling Today](https://recyclingtoday.com/article/sims-rhode-island-promet-marine-terminal); [Providence Business News](https://pbn.com/sims-metal-at-home-on-waterfront102131/)
- Schnitzer (now Radius) also ran a deep-sea ferrous export dock in Providence; Providence averaged 57,000 tonnes per month of ferrous scrap exports in 2019; Schnitzer paused that dock in April 2020 (current status not found). — [Argus](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news/2093647-schnitzer-pauses-rhode-island-ferrous-export-dock)
- Argus carried a headline that Sims suspended Rhode Island shredder operations (article 2094741, around 2020; details and current status not retrieved). — [Argus](https://www.argusmedia.com/pt/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2094741-sims-suspends-rhode-island-shredder-ops)
- Regulatory exposure: in Oct 2020 EPA and Rhode Island settled Clean Air Act claims with SMM New England (Sims) over shredder emissions, requiring $325,000 for projects in Johnston and Providence. — [EPA](https://www.epa.gov/newsreleases/epa-and-rhode-island-scrap-metal-facility-resolve-clean-air-act-claims)
- Local yards listed as offering self-service (directory data, low reliability; verify directly): Riverside Auto Salvage (Gardner, MA), Weber Auto & Truck Parts (Walpole, MA), Henry's Auto Parts (Blackstone, MA), a yard in Berlin, MA, and Bill's Auto Parts (Cumberland, RI). — [Salvage-Parts.com Massachusetts](https://www.salvage-parts.com/junk-yards/massachusetts)
- LKQ's presence found in MA is a full-service/aftermarket site (LKQ and Keystone, Maynard, MA per BBB listing); no Pick Your Part store in MA or RI appeared in results. — [BBB Massachusetts auto salvage](https://www.bbb.org/us/ma/category/auto-salvage)
- Massachusetts yards need a junk/second-hand/salvage dealer license and must meet EPA rules (directory statement, not a legal source). — [JunkyardsNearMe Massachusetts](https://junkyardsnearme.net/junkyards-massachusetts/)

### Inferences
- With one shredder/export dock dominating the Boston area, a Waltham-area yard is a price-taker on hulks, and a shredder outage directly depresses local hulk prices (shown by the Everett outages).
- The absence of national self-service chains in MA/RI is consistent with either an open niche or with land cost, zoning and winter/rust conditions making the model unattractive here; the evidence found does not distinguish between the two.

### Gaps
- No authoritative list or count of licensed auto salvage yards in Greater Boston or RI; no data on yard acreage, land prices or zoning availability near Waltham.
- Current (2026) operating status of the Sims Johnston shredder and the Radius Providence dock was not confirmed.
- No current export tonnage for Boston/Providence, and no destination data after 2011 (then: Turkey, Germany, Egypt per Providence Business News).
- No actual price paid for auto bodies in Boston in 2025-2026 (Fastmarkets series is paywalled).

## 5. Commodity prices

### Takeaway
Ferrous scrap was weak (a three-year low in Q4 2025, ~$319/ton HMS average for 2025) and choppy through 2026, so hulks are worth roughly $120-$200/ton to a yard. PGMs and aluminum were the bright spots: platinum and palladium nearly doubled in 2025, peaked in early 2026, then gave back a large part by mid-2026.

### Cited Findings
- USGS: No. 1 heavy melting scrap averaged an estimated $319.00/ton in 2025 vs $314.85/ton in 2024 (search summary; PDF returned 403 on direct fetch). — [USGS MCS 2026, Iron and Steel Scrap](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-iron-steel-scrap.pdf)
- ARA Market & Metals Q4 2025 (published Feb 5, 2026): ferrous scrap "hovered at a three-year low" in Q4 2025; values had been down 10% earlier in 2025 before stabilizing. — [ARA Market & Metals Q4 2025](https://www.a-r-a.org/news/market-metals-quarter-4-2025/)
- April 14, 2026: tight supply of shredder feed across the US East Coast, Midwest and Canada; export shredder feed at $265-275 per gross ton delivered (search summary). — [SteelOrbis](https://www.steelorbis.com/steel-prices/steel-prices-market-analyses/scrap-and-raw-materials/usec-dock-prices-cut-grades-unchanged-shredder-feed-rises-by-8gt-1447751.htm)
- 2026 direction: shredded auto scrap rose in late June 2026, fell in mid-July 2026 on subdued steel demand and ample supply, and gained $15 (+3.41%) in the week of Nov 6-12, 2026. — [ScrapMonster June 2026](https://www.scrapmonster.com/news/weekly-metal-price-report/scrap-metal-prices-weekly-market-report-june-19-25-2026-2026-6-26/99433); [ScrapMonster July 2026](https://www.scrapmonster.com/news/weekly-metal-price-report/scrap-metal-prices-weekly-market-report-july-10-16-2026-2026-7-17/99593); [ScrapMonster Nov 2026](https://www.scrapmonster.com/news/weekly-metal-price-report/steel-scrap-rises-as-copper-and-aluminum-fall-2026-11-13/100476)
- Shredded auto scrap (processed, West Coast) listed at $415/metric ton, last updated April 6, 2026 (free listing is delayed about three months). — [ScrapMonster shredded auto scrap](https://www.scrapmonster.com/scrap-prices/north-american-steel/shredded-auto-scrap-scraps/345/1/1)
- Price paid to sellers for whole junk cars, 2026: $120-$200/ton (Junk Car Medics, Feb 2026); ~$190-$195/ton national average (Junk Car Reaper, Feb 2026). — [Junk Car Medics release](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/245025/junk-car-medics-announces-updated-2026-scrap-car-pricing-and-increased-payouts-nationwide); [Junk Car Reaper](https://www.junkcarreaper.com/junk-car-prices)
- Aluminum, Q4 2025: LME spot averaged $2,618/metric ton (+7.3%); the US Midwest Premium ended 2025 at an all-time high; US tariffs of 25-50% drove domestic premiums to records and delivered aluminum cost rose over 50% during 2025; ARA expects recycled aluminum to stay high-value in 2026. — [ARA Market & Metals Q4 2025](https://www.a-r-a.org/news/market-metals-quarter-4-2025/)
- PGMs, 2023 baseline (July 2023): platinum ~$800/oz, palladium ~$1,200/oz, rhodium ~$3,500/oz, vs earlier peaks of ~$2,000 palladium and ~$30,000 rhodium. Note: the platinum figure looks low versus the general market at that time; treat as the article's rounded number. — [Auto Recycling World](https://autorecyclingworld.com/the-commodity-of-cats-full-serve-and-self-serve-yards/)
- PGMs, 2025: platinum up nearly 90% year-to-date, ~$1,560/oz by December 2025; palladium up 83% in 2025, peaking at $1,675/oz in mid-December; rhodium above-ground stocks at a 40-year low. — [ARA Market & Metals Q4 2025](https://www.a-r-a.org/news/market-metals-quarter-4-2025/)
- PGMs, Jan 20, 2026: platinum $2,346/oz, palladium $1,864/oz, rhodium $10,100/oz. — [ScrapMonster converter PGM guide, Jan 2026](https://www.scrapmonster.com/news/catalytic-converter/converter-pgm-quick-guide-jan-2026-2026-1-25/98282)
- Rhodium closed March 2026 at about $11,288/oz (search summary). — [ARS Market & Metals Q1 2026](https://www.arscars.com/?p=88348)
- PGMs, Aug 2, 2026: palladium $1,271/oz, platinum $1,646/oz, rhodium $7,825/oz — a marked fall from January-March 2026 levels. — [ScrapMonster converter prices](https://www.scrapmonster.com/catalytic-converter-scrap-price/diesel-catalytic-convertor)
- Converter economics: a typical converter holds 2-8 g of PGMs; scrap payouts run about 25-35% of spot metal value after refining costs and losses (trade guide, Jan 2026). — [ScrapMonster, PGMs in converters](https://www.scrapmonster.com/news/catalytic-converter/understanding-precious-metals-in-catalytic-converters-2026-1-15/98246)
- Forecasts made for 2026: Metals Focus expected platinum to average $2,190/oz and palladium $1,570/oz, with autocatalyst recycling up 11% to 4.4 Moz; Johnson Matthey expected platinum in deficit and small palladium and rhodium surpluses. — [IPMI / Metals Focus](https://www.ipmi.org/news/platinum-shines-brightest-bullish-2026-pgm-outlook-metals-focus); [Johnson Matthey](https://matthey.com/media/2026/johnson-matthey-publishes-2026-pgm-market-report1)

### Inferences
- Converter values in 2026 are probably well above the "slightly north of $100" average of mid-2023, given platinum roughly doubled and rhodium more than doubled from the 2023 baseline, but no 2026 per-converter average was found.
- Steel is the low-value floor of the business and aluminum/PGMs are the volatile upside; the January-to-August 2026 PGM drop (platinum -30%, palladium -32%, rhodium -23% on the cited prints) shows how quickly that upside can reverse.

### Gaps
- No Boston-specific auto-body price for any year; no clean annual 2022-2026 series for auto bodies or shredded scrap (Fastmarkets/Argus paywalled).
- Effect of steel tariffs on scrap prices: nothing found (only the aluminum tariff effect is sourced).
- No sourced 2025-2026 values for aluminum wheels per lb, lead-acid batteries, copper wiring harness, or engine/transmission cores.
- No November 2026 PGM spot prices; latest sourced print is Aug 2, 2026.

## 6. Vehicle acquisition

### Takeaway
Late-model salvage is getting more expensive and more contested: Copart reports record average selling prices and international buyers taking more than a third of US auction units and nearly half of proceeds. Low-value ELVs are bought from the public at roughly $350-$500 each, increasingly through online buyers and Copart's own Direct Buy channel.

### Cited Findings
- Copart FY2025: average selling prices for insurance vehicles grew 5.4% globally and 5.7% in the US (secondary summary). — [Autobody News](https://www.autobodynews.com/news/copart-revenue-gross-profit-net-income-up-in-q4-full-year)
- Copart fiscal Q1 2026 (reported Nov 2025): US insurance ASPs up 8.4% (6.8% globally), described as all-time highs; international buyers purchased vehicles 38% higher in value than comparable US buyers; global units sold fell 6.7%, partly because low-value units moved to a referral model called Direct Buy. — [Repairer Driven News, Nov 24, 2025](https://www.repairerdrivennews.com/2025/11/24/copart-reports-all-time-high-average-selling-prices-as-total-loss-frequency-remains-up/)
- Copart fiscal Q3 2026 call (May 2026): international buyers are more than one-third of volume sold at US Copart auctions and nearly half of auction proceeds. — [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/25/copart-cprt-q3-2026-earnings-transcript/)
- A 2025 trade article framed it as international buyers purchasing about 40% of vehicles at US auctions (slightly different from Copart's "more than one-third"). — [Collision Repair Mag](https://www.collisionrepairmag.com/business/article/15772673/price-push-copart-announces-recordhigh-parts-prices)
- ELV purchase prices from the public, 2026: median offer $350 (middle half $250-$525; 42,268 offers, Fall 2026); average payouts $400-$512 at other buyers. — [JunkUrCar index](https://news.marketersmedia.com/junk-car-prices-by-state-2026-median-offer-is-dollar350/89207453); [Junk Car Medics](https://www.junkcarmedics.com/prices); [Cash Auto Salvage](https://www.cashautosalvage.com/going-rate-junk-cars/)

### Inferences
- A used-car dealer has a real sourcing edge over a typical new entrant: trade-ins and auction rejects that are uneconomic to retail can be fed to a yard without paying auction fees or competing with export buyers. This is my inference; no source quantified dealer-sourced ELV volumes.
- Export buyers paying 38% more than domestic buyers for comparable units means a domestic dismantler is structurally outbid on the best late-model salvage; full-service models relying on late-model auction cars face rising input cost.

## 7. Trends

### Takeaway
Demand-side fundamentals are favorable: the fleet is the oldest ever (12.8 years in 2025, ~13 expected in 2026) and total-loss frequency is at a record (23.1% of claims in 2025, 23.5% through Q2 2026), which increases both parts demand and salvage supply. Offsetting risks are OEM-heavy repair practice, EV/ADAS complexity, and commodity volatility.

### Cited Findings
- Average age of US light vehicles reached 12.8 years in 2025, with a strong likelihood of reaching 13 years in 2026 (S&P Global Mobility, cited in CCC Crash Course 2026, published March 31, 2026). — [CCC Crash Course 2026 release](https://ir.cccis.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ccc-crash-course-2026-report-finds-higher-severity-and-record)
- Total-loss frequency hit a record 23.1% of all auto claims in 2025 (23.9% for non-comprehensive claims). One outlet (Carscoops) instead reports 22.8% for 2025 vs 22.1% in 2024; the 23.1% figure is the one in CCC's own release. — [CCC Crash Course 2026 release](https://ir.cccis.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ccc-crash-course-2026-report-finds-higher-severity-and-record); contradicted by [Carscoops](https://www.carscoops.com/?p=3344369)
- CCC mid-year 2026 update: through Q2 2026, 23.5% of claims across all loss categories were flagged as total losses, up 0.8 points year over year. — [Repairer Driven News, Oct 19, 2026](https://www.repairerdrivennews.com/2026/10/19/ccc-mid-year-collision-repair-trends-update-show-more-expensive-claims-total-losses/)
- Parts mix in insurer-paid repairs, 2025: OEM parts 63.5% of part replacement dollars; aftermarket a record 22.7%; average parts per repairable appraisal fell to 13.0 from 13.6 in 2024. The recycled share was not stated (search summary). — [Repairer Driven News, April 2026](https://www.repairerdrivennews.com/2026/04/10/ccc-report-shows-shift-to-drp-and-ai-appraisals/)
- Massachusetts right to repair: on Feb 11, 2025 US District Judge Denise Casper dismissed the remaining claims in the Alliance for Automotive Innovation's suit against the 2020 voter-approved vehicle data access law, leaving the law in force; the automakers' group said it might appeal (appeal status not confirmed). — [WBUR, Feb 12, 2025](https://www.wbur.org/news/2025/02/12/massachusetts-right-to-repair-carr-law-challenges-dismissed); [Repairer Driven News](https://www.repairerdrivennews.com/2025/02/17/federal-judge-cites-plain-language-of-mass-data-access-law-multiple-access-options-as-means-for-oems-to-comply/)
- EVs: battery packs are often dismantled manually because designs differ (some screwed into the chassis, some welded); untrained handling of high-voltage vehicles puts staff at risk (trade press, undated). — [American Recycler](https://americanrecycler.com/electronics-recycling/safe-dismantling-of-electric-vehicles/)
- EV battery recycling volumes are back-loaded: Redwood forecasts ~1,000 GWh of packs recovered by 2040; Cox Automotive's recycling director described the US EV recycling market as retrenching (2026 trade coverage). — [Design News](https://www.designnews.com/automotive-engineering/ev-battery-recycling-faces-supply-gap-despite-strong-recovery-rates-for-critical-materials)
- Precious-metal scarcity raises theft and security risk for yards: ARA noted rhodium stocks at a 40-year low make converter theft a high-risk issue (Feb 2026). — [ARA Market & Metals Q4 2025](https://www.a-r-a.org/news/market-metals-quarter-4-2025/)

### Inferences
- OEM 63.5% + aftermarket 22.7% leaves about 14% of insurer-paid part dollars for recycled, remanufactured and other parts combined in 2025 (my arithmetic); recycled parts are therefore a minority channel in insured collision repair, and the core customer for a junk yard remains the DIY and independent-shop market serving 10+ year old cars.
- An aging fleet plus record total losses is the strongest structural argument in favor of the business; the main counterweights are commodity exposure and rising acquisition costs.
- The MA right-to-repair law mainly benefits independent repair shops (data access); its effect on used-parts demand is indirect and unquantified.
